About FinLens & Methodology

Transparent formulas, verdict thresholds, and prototype boundaries.

1. Platform Scope

FinLens is an informational financial product discovery and EMI estimation platform built on published Indian bank and lender schedules. It helps users compare debit & credit cards, estimate loan EMIs across 9 financial goals, and check monthly repayment affordability. It does not connect to bank accounts, pull credit bureaus, or approve loans.

2. Exact Formulas Used

  • Reducing-Balance EMI: EMI = P × r × (1 + r)^n ÷ ((1 + r)^n − 1) where r = Annual Rate ÷ 12 ÷ 100. If r = 0, EMI = P ÷ n.
  • FOIR (%): (Existing EMIs + Proposed EMI) ÷ Monthly Take-Home Income × 100.
  • Disposable Income: Monthly Income − Living Expenses − Existing EMIs − Proposed EMI.

3. Affordability Verdict Thresholds

The verdict reflects the more cautious of FOIR and remaining disposable income %. CIBIL score adds an eligibility caveat only and never softens an unaffordable result.
